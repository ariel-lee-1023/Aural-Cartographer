## Mental Model (read first)
A streaming platform is an arrangement of interfaces, files, metadata, intermediaries, metrics, finance and claims about users. The promise of personal discovery is part of that arrangement, not a neutral window onto an individual's taste. Study what can be observed, how an observation was produced, and whose definition of success it serves.

The book was published in 2019 and examines earlier phases of Spotify. Its interventions and interface observations are dated cases. They do not reveal today's recommendation weights, commercial terms, catalogue, demographics or feature set.

## Frameworks & Structure
### Introduction: access, interventionism and the research object
**Teardown** uses several methods to investigate an opaque service rather than accepting a company's account as the complete research object. Interviews, documents, technical traces, interface observation and interventions illuminate different aspects and have different limits.

A black-box observation does not automatically disclose the internal mechanism. Describe what the experiment changed, what it observed and what alternative explanations remain. The book explicitly presents messy methods, failures and open questions, rather than an easily operationalized predictive model.

For this skill, these interventions are historical research data. They do not instruct the host to deploy bots, create accounts, upload test recordings, intercept traffic or manipulate stream counts. The relevant transferable discipline is to question the production of evidence. *Locator: Introduction, “Access and Interventionism,” “Platforms and Companies.”*

### 1. Where Is Spotify? History against the seamless platform story
The platform changes through financing, licensing, distribution, design, competition and self-description. A single narrative of inevitable disruption conceals this contingency. The book's periodization makes transformations visible rather than treating Spotify as one unchanged object.

**How to count:** catalogue size, registered accounts, active accounts and individual listeners are different units. Activity needs a definition and time window; duplicated recordings and nonmusical files complicate “song” counts. A growing metric cannot be interpreted until its denominator and collection process are known.

The shift toward activity- and mood-oriented experiences is historically situated. It cannot be inferred to govern every current recommendation, nor does its existence establish that listeners have ceased caring about genres. *Locator: ch. 1, particularly “How to Historicize Spotify?,” “How to Count?,” and later period sections.*

### 2. When Do Files Become Music? Aggregation and infrastructure
Music reaches the user through a layered arrangement of rights and content intermediaries, metadata, databases, software services and data flows. **Aggregation** assembles material and makes some relationships easy to access while obscuring others.

A catalogue entry is not identical to a composition, unique recording or real-world listener encounter. Version ambiguity, duplicate releases, missing credits and retrieval ranking can affect the apparent library. Preserve identifiers and version evidence rather than merging items solely by title.

The **radio loop experiment** examines actual sequences from a particular implementation and setup. Repetition in those outputs raises questions about the promise of unlimited discovery. It does not expose all internal rules or prove that every user received the same experience.

For an independent critic, inspect whether a proposed discovery route supplies a new relationship, not simply more available catalogue. Availability shapes what can be recommended and must not be mistaken for a judgment of worth. *Locator: ch. 2, “Following the Data,” “Aggregating Content,” “The Radio Loop Experiment”; related interventions.*

### 3. How Does Spotify Package Music? Playlists and subjects
Playlists have a history extending beyond streaming. Digital reaggregation can create **metacommodities**, a concept discussed through Jeremy Wade Morris: bundles that organize and repackage already commodified tracks. Sequencing, titles, images and categories help construct an experience; they are not merely containers.

**Soundtracking the lives of happy subjects** examines how interfaces prescribe normal routines, moods and aspirations. A label such as work, exercise or relaxation can shape both attention to music and a user's conception of the appropriate self. Treat a listener's actual activity as evidence, not as a category the system is entitled to impose.

**Algorithmic profiling** and the **politics of personalization** concern the relation between user traces, collective classification and an intimate user-facing story. “For you” does not mean free of assumptions about groups or commercial objectives. A recommendation's apparent relevance can coexist with a narrow conception of worthwhile listening.

For taste research, a play is not equivalent to liking, endorsement, close attention or critical judgment. A skip also needs context. Separate exposure, behavioral trace, volunteered explanation and proposed inference. *Locator: ch. 3.*

### 4. What Is the Value of Free? Markets, finance and measurement
Free access participates in markets involving audience segments, advertising, intermediaries and valuation. **Brokerage** and **arbitrage** are used analytically to understand coordination and value extraction; they do not reduce the platform to a simple seller of individual songs.

Programmatic advertising and verification arrangements help make audiences calculable. Metrics have practical effects and institutional users. The listener's understanding, autonomy and exploratory breadth need not coincide with what is easy to monetize or measure.

For Aural-Cartographer, set success independently: a justified critical view, a better historical connection, a newly audible distinction, or a revised taste hypothesis. These are user-purpose choices informed by the critique, not outcome measures experimentally validated by the book. *Locator: ch. 4, “Embedded in Finance,” “Automation—or Programmatic Advertising,” “Verification.”*

### Interventions and conclusion: limits belong with findings
The label setup, stream-tracking, data and other interventions expose both infrastructure and the difficulties of studying it. **Songblocker** is a satirical intervention reversing the expected relationship between music and ads; it is not an ordinary product recommendation.

Platform-controlled access, research dependence, experiment design and the fragility of observations constrain inference. Preserve date, setup, failure and scope beside any finding. Critical suspicion does not license invented motives, hidden parameters or universal statements about users. *Locator: intervention chapters and Conclusion.*

## Worked Example
The radio-loop investigation uses programmed listening to inspect sequences produced by Spotify Radio. Repetition becomes an observable fact within a specific setup, while the internal selection process remains uncertain. The lesson for this project is to evaluate a discovery route by its actual sequence and the relationships it opens, not by the promise that a vast catalogue must yield breadth. A route can fail even when its items are individually plausible. This is an adaptation for curation; the skill does not reproduce the experiment. *Locator: ch. 2, “The Radio Loop Experiment.”*

## Decision Rules & Judgment
- When a recommendation is personalized, ask what evidence and objective personalization actually uses.
- When interpreting play or skip data, preserve ambiguous exposure and context rather than silently assigning preference.
- When a route repeats the same territory, change the relation being explored, not merely the artist names.
- When a playlist labels a listener's mood or identity, treat it as a platform classification, not a psychological fact.
- When using historical platform findings, name their period and verify any current operational claim separately.
- When citing a black-box experiment, report observations and competing explanations before claiming a mechanism.
- When designing this critic's output, choose understanding and meaningful exploration over engagement volume.

## Key Takeaways
1. Discovery is mediated by infrastructure and commercial classification.
2. Metrics require definitions and do not transparently reveal taste.
3. Personalization can shape the subject it claims merely to serve.
4. A large catalogue does not guarantee a broad route through it.
5. Critical analysis must retain evidence limits and historical dates.
