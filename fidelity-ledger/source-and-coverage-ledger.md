This is a maintainer record, not runtime knowledge. The user supplied twelve books and a separate English role brief. ## Extraction and scope

The first-party extraction runtime processed exactly eleven Markdown files and the Frith PDF with no failed sources. The bundled Python runtime supplied PDF backends. Text mode was appropriate for the predominantly prose corpus, but music notation, tabular OCR and figures are limited. No pitch-level claim was inferred from a damaged diagram. Markdown page counts produced by the extractor are length heuristics, not physical pagination; references therefore do not report them as book page counts. Frith has 364 actual PDF pages, with printed pagination distinguished from PDF indices.

Automatic chapter detection undercounted converted academic headings. Counts were recovered from contents and heading probes; counts in the manifest represent the declared substantive structure, not the number of generated headings. Supplementary fronts/appendices are treated explicitly below. English is the principal language of all substantive supplied texts, including the Hennion translation, so the runtime default is English. The build conversation remained Chinese as requested by the project instructions.

The source text totals approximately 2.57 million script-density tokens. It was queried in bounded excerpts rather than fully read. Reading statistics cover logged semantic excerpts; preliminary metadata/contents probes are excluded because their mixed, sometimes truncated outputs do not support an exact total. The ledger is not a claim of complete source reading. Source-local lines refer to the exact extracted text, with hashes of original inputs in the manifest. References supply chapter/section locators portable without the private corpus.

## Cross-source commitments and real tensions

| Core commitment | Support and distinction |
|---|---|
| Evaluate means and achievement, separately from predicted liking | User brief + Frith; Moore supplies interpretive analysis, Zak supplies record construction. This is the project's procedure, not a single author's named rubric. |
| Aesthetic project is an interpretation, not known private intention | Moore's intention/reception boundary; Zak's practitioners and session histories can supply separate production evidence. |
| Keep attachment, use and expectation distinct | Hennion's reflexive mediation is not DeNora's situated use account, and neither is reducible to Huron's psychological proposal. |
| Social explanation improves but does not replace criticism | Frith and Hennion resist reduction; Wilson's self-examination remains in tension with Roth/n+1's criticism of sociological displacement. Sterne also questions compulsory pluralism. |
| Organize historical relations with their evidence | Brackett's category history, Lena's genre organization, Wald's anti-canonical reconstruction and Katz's technological mechanisms answer different questions. |
| Discovery should widen understanding | User brief determines the aim; Spotify Teardown questions metrics and personalization, but does not experimentally validate this skill's recommendation formats. |
| Keep counterexamples and revisions | User brief + Hennion/DeNora's attention to trials and contexts; the table and comparison workflow are authored operational syntheses. |
| No invented listening | Explicit user purpose. Published analysis is attributed; unavailable sonic data remains unavailable. |

## Budget and density decisions

References are intentionally well below the provisional chapter-based targets, while retaining conditions, judgment rules and one attributed worked example each. Targets are not floors: padding to 6,300–11,094 tokens per book would burden a consultation with lower-priority historical detail. Realized references are about 2,000–2,900 estimated tokens each. The comparison to these targets is recorded in validation and the manifest; this build must not be cited as evidence that all source material was compressed losslessly.

The density heuristic is only a calibration aid. “Named item” counts are interpretive, especially in historical monographs, so no artificial count was manufactured to achieve the 50–70-token band. The more meaningful editorial test here was whether a retained distinction states what to examine, what it changes, and where an inference fails. Behavioral gain remains unmeasured.

## Attribution and currency

Source examples are paraphrases with locators. Fabbri's genre rules in Frith, Gracyk/Goodman's autographic discussion in Zak, and Morris's metacommodity in Spotify Teardown retain nested attribution. Wilson's contributors are identified by name. Huron's older empirical and neural proposals are not silently promoted into current consensus. Platform cases are explicitly historical. No source pages, lyrics, audio, scanned figures, or complete extraction text are published.
