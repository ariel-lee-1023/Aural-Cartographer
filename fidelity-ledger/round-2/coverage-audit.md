## Targeted corrections that changed the candidate

- Straus §2.2 was checked visually: the fourth-edition normal-form tie break allows packing toward either end. The traditional always-bottom-packed rule was not silently substituted.
- Caplin's half cadence ends on a root-position dominant triad in this account. The source's V7 boundary was retained rather than broadened to a generic “any dominant ending.”
- Laitz's tonicization/modulation contrast retains cadential confirmation and what follows, rather than a bar-count threshold.
- Rosen uses “recapitulation” broadly in the printed p. 74 discussion, including coda; the reference warns against conflating this with Caplin's terminology.
- Cook's bibliography/front matter and Romantic Generation's missing contents were recovered from sibling PDFs. Neither a damaged Markdown TOC nor automated heading count was treated as the true chapter count.
- Taruskin front matter includes 2010 copyright; Ross identifies a 2012 eBook. The manifest distinguishes those supplied witnesses from the works' original publication dates.
