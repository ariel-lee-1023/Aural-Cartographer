Bounded audit samples were chosen to test answer-changing exceptions outside the initial emphasis on flow mechanics, sample transformation and R&B musical grammar. Line ranges are local to the private source rendition, whose source hash and extraction provenance are in the manifest. They are location records, not redistributed source text.
